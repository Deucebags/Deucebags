Deucebags privacy

Deucebags has no ads or tracking, and accounts are optional. Here's exactly what it stores and where.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, results, players, league, achievements, profile and friends list are saved inside the app on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app or clearing your browser's data removes it.

Videos and photos

The camera and microphone are only used while you're filming or scanning a friend code. Videos and clips stay in the app until you save or delete them. When you save, a copy goes to your phone's own storage. Your profile photo is saved on your phone only.

What you choose to share

Friend codes and match results travel inside the links you send. Anyone with one of those links can see what's in it: your name, photo, bio and socials for a friend code, or the match score for a result. Nothing is shared unless you send it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, your results, profile and friends list are also saved to your Deucebags account on Google Firebase, stored in London, so they sync between your devices. Your results stay private to you, apart from matches you play with friends you've connected with, which are sent to them. Your profile card (name, username, photo, bio, padel details and headline stats such as matches played, win rate, rating and achievements) can be seen by other Deucebags users who search for your username. If you join a shared group, matches tagged with that group, and live scores while you're scoring one, are shared with its members. Your email is used to sign you in and is never shown to anyone. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Account and sync.

Other services the app uses

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its QR code tool from Cloudflare. Like any website, those services can see your device's IP address when the app loads. They don't receive any of your scores, videos or profile.
